Holdfast privacy policy

For the Holdfast Android app (package com.emblemcards.game), version 0.1.0. Last updated: November 5, 2026.

Holdfast is a single-player card game that plays entirely offline. There is no account, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking. The app has no internet permission and asks for no access to your data, so it cannot send your information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Holdfast saves the battle you are playing so you can quit and resume it. That save holds only game state: the board, your hand and deck, and the turn. It is kept in the app's local storage on your device and is never transmitted. Android's own backup may include it if you have device backup turned on.

What we do not collect

Holdfast does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID or any other personal information. It contains no third-party SDKs for ads or analytics.

Children

Holdfast does not collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Your choices and deletion

To delete the saved battle, finish or quit it from the menu, clear Holdfast's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. There is no online account or server copy to delete.

Earlier versions

Holdfast replaces Emblem Cards, an earlier prototype released under the same package. Emblem Cards had an optional online mode that sent a random player ID, a display name and game moves to a server the player chose. Holdfast has no online mode.

Changes
